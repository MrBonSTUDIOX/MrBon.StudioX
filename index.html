About

MrBon Studio X

An independent game studio.

The studio is named after its first character: MrBon, a pixel-art teddy bear with a blue collar who is a lot less friendly than he looks.

We make original games, characters and worlds, and try out odd ideas along the way. Survive from MrBon is the first game you can download. BonSquad is the next project.

The characters, worlds and ideas here are our own.

What we work on

  • Games
  • Original characters & universes
  • Interactive experiences
  • Creative experiments

The idea

Every project here started as an idea. The goal is simple: turn ideas into things you can actually play.

What we’re making

Playable

Survive from MrBon

A 2D survival horror game with one rule: don’t be in the room when the door gives way. You get 30 seconds to lock yourself in and reinforce the door. Then MrBon comes looking. He picks a locked door and starts breaking it down. Hurt him enough and he retreats to heal, but he always comes back.
